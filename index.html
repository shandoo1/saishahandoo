Identity

University of Maryland alumna, having double majored in Information Systems and Operations Management with a concentration in Business Analytics. Technical enough to understand constraints, business-minded enough to navigate tradeoffs, user-obsessed enough to care deeply about the experience that ultimately reaches the person on the other side of the screen.
Why Product
"Fix the experience upstream."
+
I've always been the person who notices when something doesn't work, not just that it's broken, but why it's frustrating for the person using it. At Moore, I traced 30% of escalations back to a single ambiguous design decision. I redesigned it. Resolution time dropped 20%. That moment crystallized it: the highest-leverage thing you can do is fix the experience upstream.
The Journey
"Cross-cultural instinct isn't a soft skill. It's a design superpower."
+
I grew up across five countries, which means I naturally think about how the same product lands differently depending on who's using it. I don't default to the assumed user. I build for everyone.
What Makes Me, Me
"Out under the stars with a camera, baking something with too much cardamom."
+
When I'm not thinking about products, I'm out under the stars with a camera, running trails, baking something that probably has too much cardamom, or playing my ukulele badly and confidently. I'm obsessively outdoorsy. Travel is less a hobby and more a way of collecting perspectives. Every country I've lived in shows up in how I design.
Why I'm Different
Five countries, three majors, one mission. Connect the dots everyone else skipped.
+
Most people study one thing. I study the space between things. Between the technical and the human, between the business goal and the user's reality, between what a product says it does and what it actually feels like to use.
